My Story

This Is Why I Started Dad's Day In Court.

I'm Clinton, a father who went through the family court system and came out of the experience with a different perspective on fatherhood, custody, and the challenges families face when relationships break down.

My journey was personal. The consequences affected my family, my relationship with my children, and my understanding of how difficult the process can become.

The Journey

The Fight to Remain a Father

When my family situation changed, I faced challenges that tested me as a father. Navigating custody proceedings was not just about legal decisions. It was about my children, our relationship, and my ability to remain present in their lives.

In November 2023, my custody journey reached a critical point. What followed became one of the most difficult chapters of my life.

I continued fighting for my children, determined to protect my relationship with them and work toward an outcome that allowed me to remain their father in a meaningful way.

I ultimately won custody, but the experience left me with questions that extended beyond my own case.

Why Dad's Day In Court Exists

Real Experiences Deserve Greater Attention.

My experience made me realize that the challenges people face in family court deserve greater attention.

I created Dad's Day In Court to share my story, discuss what fathers can experience during custody disputes, and encourage honest conversations about the family court system.

This platform is not about assuming every case is the same or that one parent is always right. Every family has its own circumstances, and children's well-being must remain central.

It's about sharing real experiences, asking difficult questions, and helping people better understand the human impact of family court.

Looking Ahead

My Story Is Just the Beginning

I cannot change what my family went through, but I can use my experience to help others understand the challenges that can arise when families enter the court system.

Through my videos and the conversations we share here, I want to bring attention to these issues and encourage people to look more closely at how the system works and where improvements may be needed.
